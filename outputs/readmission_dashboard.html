Key findings

  • 11.39% of eligible diabetic stays (11,314) were readmitted within 30 days, about $172M at the AHRQ average cost.
  • Readmission risk climbs with every prior inpatient stay: 8.59% with none vs 37.13% with 5+ (4.32x). Adjusted OR per extra stay = 1.46.
  • 8.75% of patients (3+ stays) account for 52.32% of all readmissions.
  • Discharge to rehab / long-term care has a 24.21% readmission rate vs 9.3% for home (adjusted OR 3.69). SNF discharges alone create ~801 excess readmissions.
  • Only 16.95% of stays had an HbA1c test. For diabetes-primary first stays, tested patients were readmitted 7.23% vs 10.17% untested (p = 0.0002).
  • The standard LACE index only reaches AUC 0.572; the gradient boosting model reaches 0.689 on unseen patients (gain 95% CI 0.103 to 0.132).
  • Calling the top 20% of discharges catches 40.78% of readmissions with the model vs 27.45% with LACE - about 49% more with the same staff.
  • At $500/patient and a 20% effect, the break-even risk is 16.45%. Enrolling the model's top 15% nets $412,300 per 10,000 discharges; enrolling everyone loses $1,549,962.
  • The program stays profitable in 19 of 20 cost / effect scenarios tested.
